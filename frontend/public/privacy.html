Privacy Policy for Saul Legal AI Assistant

Effective Date: November 2026

Saul ("we", "our", or "us") provides a legal assistance artificial intelligence platform. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you access our application.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in using Google OAuth, we only receive your basic profile information (such as your name, email address, and profile picture). We do not request access to your private emails, Google Drive, or contacts.

2. How We Use Information

We use your basic profile information solely to authenticate your identity, display your avatar/name in the legal consultation portal, and associate your chat sessions with your profile.

3. Data Confidentiality

Chat transcripts, evidentiary photos, and consultation records are handled confidentially and are not sold or transferred to advertising networks.
